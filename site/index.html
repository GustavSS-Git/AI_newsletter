Business & Funding

OpenAI to acquire developer tooling startup Pierre Computer

OpenAI announced plans to acquire Pierre Computer Company, which runs a cloud code-storage service and builds open-source libraries. Two of them, Trees and Diffs, already power the interface of OpenAI's Codex coding tool. OpenAI says the deal is meant to improve Git workflows when developers and AI agents write code together, and it will keep Pierre's open-source projects open. Terms were not disclosed, and The Decoder reports the deal still needs regulatory approval.

Sources: SiliconANGLE AI, The Decoder, Axios, tech-insider.org

Policy & Regulation

Voters approve about two dozen data center restrictions in midterms

Of 41 data center measures on ballots across 10 states, about two dozen that restrict or block development appeared to pass, according to Ballotpedia via Axios. Democrat Gina Hinojosa won the Texas governor's race after campaigning for a data center construction moratorium until the state passes new legislation. Texas accounts for 41% of new US data center capacity in 2028, according to SemiAnalysis. In NBC News and SSRS exit polls, 56% of respondents called AI and data centers a decisive or important factor, the lowest of five topics surveyed.

Sources: The Verge AI, Ars Technica AI, MIT Technology Review AI, CNBC Technology
