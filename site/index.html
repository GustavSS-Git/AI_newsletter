Research

Anthropic releases open-source FlashProve to verify which model wrote an output

Anthropic published research on FlashProve, a system that uses cryptographic proofs to confirm an output came from a specific AI model. Labs would publish a "fingerprint" of each model that does not reveal its weights. Anthropic says a single H100 GPU generates proofs at about 110 tokens per second for a 400-billion-parameter model, at roughly 70-95 times the GPU time of running the model. The code is under the Apache 2.0 license, and Anthropic says it has no plans to deploy the system.

Why it matters: Such proofs could let users and evaluators check that a provider is not secretly swapping in a different model.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, anthropic.com, PYMNTS.com, Benzinga

Policy & Regulation

Google will disable AI tools in schools by default, cutting Search for opt-outs

Google said Tuesday it will turn off AI tools for K-12 students next year unless administrators enable them, covering Gemini, NotebookLM, Gemini in Classroom and Search. Schools that restrict AI entirely will lose access to Google Search, because AI features are integrated into it. Google says the change follows consultations with schools and researchers. DuckDuckGo is pitching schools on switching their default search engine after the backlash.

Why it matters: Schools that want to keep Google Search must accept its AI features.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, Ars Technica AI, nytimes.com, WBAY
