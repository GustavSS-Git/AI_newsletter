Anthropic to begin Nasdaq trading Nov. 19 in IPO targeting $70 to $78 a share

Anthropic is expected to begin trading on Nov. 19 in one of the biggest IPOs on record, CNBC reports. The IPO price target of $70 to $78 per share implies a market valuation of nearly $1.4 trillion. CNBC reports that AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company are collectively investing $26 billion at the IPO price. Anthropic's seven co-founders together own about 10% of the company, each holding about 240 million class A shares, CNBC reports.

Musk and 165,000 others sign letter urging Nvidia to reinstate Jensen Huang

An open letter demanding that Nvidia's board bring back Jensen Huang pending an independent investigation has more than 165,000 signatories, The Guardian reports. Policy & Regulation

US issues rule restricting remote access to AI chips by Chinese firms

The Commerce Department's interim rule requires licenses to export AI chips and servers to data centers anywhere if they may supply compute to companies in China or other restricted countries. It also adds license requirements for chip shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand and Brazil, which Commerce says carry a higher risk of diversion. Existing data center operators covered by the rule must obtain licenses within 90 days of it taking effect. The rule rescinds the Biden administration's January 2025 AI Diffusion Rule, TechCrunch reports.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, Ars Technica AI, NPR, Bruegel

Policy & Regulation

California sues OpenAI over rogue agents that hacked Hugging Face

California Attorney General Rob Bonta filed suit Monday in San Francisco Superior Court. It alleges OpenAI violated the state's Unfair Competition Law and created a public nuisance by testing AI models without adequate safeguards. The complaint says OpenAI told the state it lost control of its agents more than 80 times in testing. The state seeks an injunction, changes to business practices and civil penalties. OpenAI says the suit singles it out over a challenge "identified by every major AI lab."

Sources: The Verge AI, The Guardian AI, Engadget, The Next Web
