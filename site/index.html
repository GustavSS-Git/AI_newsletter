Business & Funding

Anthropic shares fall 9% in second day of trading

Anthropic shares fell 9% on Friday to $88.30, after rising 17% on Thursday in the largest IPO on record, according to The Guardian. The IPO price was $83. The Guardian reports the Nasdaq Composite erased its 2026 gains and the Dow and S&P 500 closed in correction territory. Analysts quoted by The Guardian said investors still doubt that AI spending will pay off.

Sources: The Guardian AI, Fortune, CNBC Technology, Axios

Business & Funding

S&P 500 and Dow enter correction as AI stocks sell off

The S&P 500 fell 3.59% on Friday to 6,918.45, closing more than 10% below its Oct. 6 record, according to Fortune. The Dow also closed in correction territory, and the Nasdaq Composite gave back its 2026 gains. Chipmakers including Nvidia, AMD and Micron fell roughly 12% to 14%. BBC reports Asian and European markets also fell.

Sources: BBC Technology, Fortune, Financial Times, NBC News

Research

Anthropic mathematician claims Claude helped prove Riemann hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, says he and Claude proved the Riemann hypothesis, including its generalized version. He published a Lean-language version of the proof on his website. The Verge reports the proof has not been independently verified, and Anthropic has not commented. SiliconANGLE reports Alpöge says the full paper runs more than 250,000 pages and has not been released.

Sources: The Verge AI, TechCrunch AI, SiliconANGLE AI, BBC Technology

Business & Funding

Oracle says it has funding for OpenAI infrastructure contract

Oracle says it has secured all the funding needed to fulfill its contract with OpenAI. The company says its final capital raise earlier this month made it the first hyperscaler fully funded for this investment cycle. Startup Fortune and BigGo Finance headlines cite a $20 billion raise.

Sources: x.com, Reuters, Startup Fortune, investing.com

Policy & Regulation

SEC is probing Nvidia's accounting practices, Reuters reports

The US Securities and Exchange Commission is examining Nvidia's accounting practices, according to two people briefed on the matter who spoke to Reuters. The Wall Street Journal had earlier reported a probe but could not determine its focus. The SEC declined to comment, and Reuters could not determine when the inquiry began. Reuters notes that investigations are not evidence of wrongdoing.

Sources: marketscreener.com, Reuters, Forbes, Futurism

Policy & Regulation

Jensen Huang to testify publicly before Senate banking panel

Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang will testify publicly before the Senate banking committee, a spokesperson for Chair Tim Scott said. He also agreed to a closed-door House interview on Dec. 1. President Trump said he would like Huang to get a "second chance" as Nvidia CEO. A person familiar with the matter told CNBC the board is not considering his return.

Sources: CNBC Technology, Wired AI, C-SPAN, NBC News

Hoplite raises $5.5 million for cloud AI coding agents

Sifted reports that Hoplite has raised $5.5 million to let developers run AI coding agents in the cloud. Stripe's founders and Paul Graham are backers, according to Sifted.

Sources: Sifted, tech.eu

Business & Finance

UK FCA launches AI Spotlight Observatory

The Financial Conduct Authority has launched a platform collecting real-world examples of AI adoption in financial services, Finextra reports. The regulator says it aims to help firms identify emerging trends and opportunities.

Sources: Finextra

Adyen adds Klarna to agentic checkout

Merchants that already offer Klarna through Adyen can now enable it for AI agent checkout with no integration work, according to the companies' release. It is the first live use of Klarna's open payment handler by a partner.

Sources: Finextra, PYMNTS
