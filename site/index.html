Duffy blames FAA outage on contractor's possible faulty router

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed Friday's FAA outage on copper-to-fiber wiring work at a remote facility, according to the Wall Street Journal. He said contractor L3Harris may have installed a "faulty router." The Journal reports that work at the single location caused instability, and authorities ordered a nationwide ground stop. Airlines continue to cancel flights as they reset operations.

Sources: Wired AI, The Wall Street Journal, The Washington Post, UkrMedia News

Business & Funding

WSJ report says Nvidia funded customers to keep GPU demand high

The Wall Street Journal reports that Nvidia financed AI startups and cloud providers that bought its GPUs, according to TechSpot and TechPowerUp's accounts of the report. The aim, the report says, was to project high demand and absorb an oversupply of TSMC chips. In an August meeting, CFO and interim CEO Colette Kress pushed to scale back such deals, including a $250 billion arrangement with OpenAI, and Jensen Huang rebuked her, the report says. Nvidia later slowed and paused some financing deals. Huang has since resigned.

Sources: techspot.com, techpowerup.com, shattered.io, Notebookcheck

Other

OpenAI folds robotics team into research and applied groups

OpenAI reorganized its robotics team. Researchers are moving to the main research group under Chief Research Officer Mark Chen, and the applied robotics and hardware teams are joining Greg Brockman's Applied organization. OpenAI told The Decoder that robotics work continues. The Decoder reports that OpenAI appears to believe general-purpose models such as GPT-6 Astra can control robots directly.

Sources: The Decoder, Startup Fortune, The Times of India, Tech in Asia

Business & Funding

Anthropic's commitments near $901 billion days before IPO, Bloomberg reports

Anthropic has committed nearly $901 billion, mostly to compute and chips, according to Bloomberg. About $71 billion comes due by September 2027. CEO Dario Amodei called compute "the deepest business dilemma" the company faces at a JPMorgan Chase event on Friday. Anthropic plans to raise up to $64.4 billion in its Nov. 19 IPO and $26 billion in a private placement, Bloomberg reports. Company filings show annualized revenue of $81 billion at the end of October.

Sources: The Decoder, CleanTechnica, Yahoo Finance

Policy & Regulation

Italy fines Meta €805 million over Android photo access

Italy's data protection agency fined Meta Platforms €805 million ($887 million), Reuters reports. The agency alleges Meta accessed Android users' photos and videos without their consent or without informing them.

Sources: Reuters, DigitBin

Business & Funding

Nvidia director Mark Stevens sold about $946 million in stock

Nvidia board director Mark Stevens sold roughly 4.2 million shares in three batches in the third quarter of 2026, according to SEC filings cited by Yahoo Finance. The sales, between Aug. 31 and Sept. 18, brought in about $946 million. Yahoo Finance reports he had no prearranged trading plan and still holds about 27.5 million shares. Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on Nov. 12.

Sources: finance.yahoo.com
