Policy & Regulation

Voters back data center limits in 33 of 45 local ballot measures

According to The Verge, 33 of 45 local ballot measures on data centers will restrict development, based on a Redbud Consulting tally as of Wednesday morning. In NBC News polling on Election Day, 76% of voters said they opposed data centers in their communities. Winners including Texas Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa and Michigan Sen.-elect Abdul El-Sayed support moratoriums. In NBC News and SSRS exit polls, 56% called AI and data centers a decisive or important factor in their vote, the lowest of five issues surveyed, The Decoder reports.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, The Verge AI, Ars Technica AI, MIT Technology Review AI

Products

Google to cut Search for schools that block its AI features

Google announced new AI tools for schools and controls letting administrators turn AI off. Chris Phillips, Google's vice president for education, told the New York Times that schools that ban AI will lose access to Search, because "search and AI are deeply integrated." New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani and former FTC chair Lina Khan accused Google of using its search monopoly to push AI into schools. Google also said Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Search will be off for K-12 student accounts until administrators turn them on.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, Ars Technica AI, The Guardian AI, CNBC Technology
