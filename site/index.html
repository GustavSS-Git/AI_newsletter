Business & Funding

Anthropic expected to debut on Nasdaq Thursday at $70-$78 a share

Anthropic is expected to list on the Nasdaq on Thursday, with the IPO price currently expected at $70 to $78 a share, CNBC reports. Yahoo Finance reports the offering was oversubscribed seven times. AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company are collectively investing $26 billion at the IPO price. Anthropic's prospectus shows spending commitments above $900 billion and a net loss of $104 billion for the first nine months of 2026. CNBC says the IPO could raise more than $90 billion, on top of $132 billion in existing cash.

Sources: CNBC Technology, Bloomberg, Yahoo Finance, Reuters

Trump administration plans to name AI 'national champions' including Nvidia and OpenAI

The Trump administration plans to name Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic among 24 or more AI "national champions," the Wall Street Journal reports, citing sources. The program is meant to help US AI companies win business overseas.

Sources: The Wall Street Journal, Crypto Briefing, breakingthenews.net, moneycontrol.com

Other

Jensen Huang discusses return as Nvidia CEO, FT reports

Jensen Huang has discussed a possible return as Nvidia's CEO with allies, days after he resigned, the Financial Times reports. Nvidia investors Brad Gerstner and Gavin Baker wrote to the board urging a slow search that includes "the potential return of Jensen Huang." Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Sunday that Nvidia should look outside the company for its next chief. Nvidia reports results Tuesday under interim CEO Colette Kress, and a board inquiry run by Wachtell Lipton is ongoing.

Sources: The Verge AI, The Next Web, Reuters, Financial Times

Policy & Regulation

Bessent urges Nvidia to pick outside CEO, calls for 'dramatic shakeup'

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Fox News that Nvidia's board should look outside the company for its next CEO and that the company needs a "dramatic shakeup." He questioned whether Nvidia pressed "seemingly illicit sales" to boost revenue and earnings. He said a bank with similar compliance failures would see many executives removed by its regulator. Bessent also said Treasury and other agencies had favored placing Megaspeed on the export blacklist for months, but "Commerce has the pen."

Sources: CNBC Technology, Axios, Fortune, shattered.io

Policy & Regulation

Megaspeed arranged nearly $12 billion of Nvidia chip orders for China

Megaspeed arranged nearly $12 billion in orders for Nvidia chips from Chinese companies between March 2024 and June 2025, according to documents reviewed by the Wall Street Journal. The Singapore-based cloud company is linked to Alice Huang, who is the former partner of ousted Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. Megaspeed says she left the company on Aug. 30, 2025, and that reports about its business are not supported by verifiable evidence. The company says it has hired outside counsel for an internal investigation.

Sources: CNBC Technology, The Decoder, BBC Technology, The Times

Models

Alibaba releases Qwen4-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter flagship model

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max, which has 2.4 trillion parameters and a 1-million-token context window. The company says it beats Qwen3.8-Max with up to about 50% fewer tokens, and beats Fable 5.1 and Kimi K3.1 Pro on coding benchmarks, according to Bloomberg. The model is not open weight, though Alibaba plans to release weights for smaller Qwen4 models. Artificial Analysis gives Qwen4-Max an Intelligence Index score of 43, ahead of Qwen3.8 Max at about 67% lower cost per task but behind Kimi K3.1 Pro.

Sources: scmp.com, artificialanalysis.ai, qwen.ai, Bloomberg

Mosaico raised €7.5 million in seed funding, Sifted reports. The startup helps robotics companies manage the sensor data needed to develop AI-powered robots.

Starling founder Anne Boden is building Lockwood, a bank for AI agents

Anne Boden, who stepped down as Starling's CEO three years ago, is building Lockwood, a digital bank designed to let AI agents make payments for customers, Sifted reports. She is investing proceeds from her Starling share sales in it.

Sources: Sifted
