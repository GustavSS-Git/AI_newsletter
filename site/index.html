Democratic midterm sweep raises odds of stricter AI rules

Democrats may end up with 56 Senate seats and as many as 261 House seats, according to Transformer's count. Transformer reports that more than 30 ballot measures restricting data centers passed. Goldman Sachs warned investors to expect "short-term data center moratoria," potentially affecting about 45% of planned US data center capacity expansion. Bloomberg and Yahoo Finance report that tech stocks reacted unevenly, with Microsoft up and Tesla and Nvidia down.

Why it matters: Transformer argues that larger Democratic majorities make a stronger frontier AI bill in early 2027 more likely, with data center limits already advancing at the state and local level.

OpenAI agrees to acquire developer-tools maker Pierre Computer

OpenAI will acquire Pierre Computer Company, maker of Code Storage and the Trees and Diffs libraries already used in its Codex app. OpenAI says it wants to build on Git workflows for coding agents and bring Code Storage's features into Codex. Pierre's open-source projects will stay open source, and current Code Storage customers can keep using it after closing. The deal is subject to customary conditions, including regulatory approvals.

Why it matters: OpenAI is extending Codex from code generation into source control and code review.

Sources: OpenAI, Axios, https://tech-insider.org/, The Tech Buzz

Research

Anthropic releases FlashProve prototype to verify which model wrote a response

Anthropic published FlashProve, an open-source prototype that uses cryptographic proofs to show a response came from a specific AI model without revealing its weights. Anthropic says a single H100 GPU generates proofs at about 110 tokens per second for a 400-billion-parameter model, at roughly 70 to 95 times the GPU time of running the model. The code, specification and Lean proofs are on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license. TechCrunch reports there are no plans to deploy it at Anthropic or any other lab.

Why it matters: The work targets a gap users face today: they cannot check that a provider served the model it claims, for example after a cost-saving swap.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, anthropic.com, NewsCord, shattered.io

OCC issues AI supervisory framework for bank examiners

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency issued a framework for its bank examination staff on banks' use of AI, according to Finextra. Reuters describes it as the regulator's first AI guidance for examiners.

Why it matters: Bank examiners now have formal guidance for supervising how banks use AI.

Sources: Finextra, Reuters, PYMNTS.com, Decrypt

Products

Google to turn off AI tools for K-12 students by default

Google says it will disable Gemini, NotebookLM, Gemini in Classroom and AI in Search for K-12 students next year unless administrators turn them on. Schools that restrict AI altogether will lose access to Google Search, because its AI features are built in, a Google executive told The New York Times. DuckDuckGo is pitching its search engine to schools in response.

Why it matters: Schools that want Google Search must accept its AI features, because Google does not offer a way to separate them.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, Ars Technica AI, nytimes.com, WBAY
