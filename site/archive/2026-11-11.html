Business & Funding

Anthropic sets IPO range at $70 to $78 per share

Anthropic is offering 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT." The public offering could raise up to $64.4 billion, and a separate $26 billion private placement includes anchor investors such as Google, Nvidia and Microsoft. At the top of the range, the company would be valued at $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis, according to The Guardian; The Decoder, citing the share count, puts the figure at up to $1.02 trillion. The prospectus shows Q3 revenue of $16.96 billion, up 47% from the prior quarter, and compute commitments of nearly $900 billion, per The Decoder. The Guardian reports trading is expected to begin next week.

Sources: CNBC Technology, TechCrunch AI, BBC Technology, The Guardian AI

Reports link Alice Huang to $7.5 billion in Nvidia chip sales to China

Tom's Hardware and Gizmodo report that Alice Huang was reportedly involved in securing more than $7.5 billion of Nvidia AI chip imports into China. Tom's Hardware says this covered 240,000 chips, mostly H100 and H200. The New York Times describes her as an alleged Chinese spy and Megaspeed founder. Author Desmond Shum argues she is more likely a commercial fixer than a spy.

Sources: desmondshum.substack.com, The New York Times, Financial Times, Tom's Hardware

Business & Funding

Nvidia hires Wachtell Lipton to investigate CEO Jensen Huang

Nvidia's independent board members retained Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz and former acting U.S. Attorney Joon H. Kim to investigate CEO Jensen Huang. Huang admitted to an affair with Alice Huang, whom U.S. intelligence agencies reportedly link to Chinese intelligence, according to The New York Times and Financial Times. Nvidia stock fell 6% on Tuesday after dropping more than 4% on Monday, CNBC reports. The board said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.

Sources: CNBC Technology, Politico Europe Tech, BBC Technology, TechCrunch AI

Products

Anthropic launches Claude Sheets, Whiteboards and Data for office workers

Anthropic unveiled Claude Sheets, Claude Whiteboards and Claude Meetings in beta at a San Francisco event, according to The Verge. Sheets and Whiteboards are available on all paid plans, and Meetings on Teams and Enterprise plans. Claude Data, formerly Dashboards, is now generally available on all paid plans. Models

Google makes Gemini 4 Carbon generally available

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon to paying developers and enterprises after a two-week early-access phase. Google says it is built for tasks such as complex multi-step coding and scientific research, and that Gemini 4 Argon remains the better choice for most software engineering work. Introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, according to The Verge. Google also previewed a "/swarm" feature in Antigravity that turns one prompt into a team of agents.

Sources: The Verge AI, The Decoder, Google DeepMind, VentureBeat

Europe

Eight countries and AI labs sign Paris Call on AI safety

Eight countries, AI companies including OpenAI, Anthropic and Mistral, and civil-society groups signed the nonbinding "Paris Call for AI Safety and Security," The Decoder reports. Companies commit to measures including shutdown mechanisms for models under their control; Germany did not sign.

Sources: The Decoder, MLex, mlq.ai, The Globe and Mail

EU draft Digital Fairness Act targets addictive design and chatbots

A draft seen by Bloomberg would require companies to let adults switch off features that encourage compulsive use, and would target AI systems that simulate personal relationships. Fines could reach 6% of worldwide annual revenue, and the rules are set to be presented Wednesday.

Sources: Insurance Journal, Rapporteur (ex-Euractiv), Bloomberg
