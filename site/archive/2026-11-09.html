Nvidia board opens investigation into CEO Jensen Huang's ties to suspected Chinese informant

Nvidia's board has started an independent investigation after reports that CEO Jensen Huang had a relationship with Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore data center operator Megaspeed. Axios and Reuters saw a draft email in which Huang admits he was unfaithful to his wife and takes full responsibility. The New York Times reported that US officials concluded she was a Chinese intelligence asset. The Chinese embassy denied the espionage allegations. Nvidia's board says it has no reason to believe US national security or company secrets were compromised and that it has confidence in Huang. President Trump called Huang "an honorable guy," while Republican lawmakers asked him for a closed-door briefing to the House foreign affairs and intelligence committees.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, The Verge AI, The Guardian AI, CNBC Technology

Goldman Sachs limits Claude Code and GitHub to its engineers

Goldman Sachs updated internal guidance in October to restrict Anthropic's Claude Code and Microsoft's GitHub to its engineers, Bloomberg News reported, after a review of productivity and risk concerns. Traders and bankers had used the tools to monitor client flows and model market moves. Strats, who work across trading, quantitative research and software engineering, keep access. A Goldman spokesperson said the bank makes certain AI tools available "to determine the best use cases." Goldman and Morgan Stanley are lead underwriters on Anthropic's IPO, according to its S-1 filing.

Sources: thestateofai.com
