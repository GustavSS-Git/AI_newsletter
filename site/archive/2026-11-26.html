Situational Awareness hedge fund lost 65% in third quarter

The Wall Street Journal reports that Leopold Aschenbrenner told investors his fund, Situational Awareness, lost 65% after fees in the third quarter. The letter says the fund is up 88.5% after fees for the year through September. The Journal says the fund nearly went out of business after steep losses this summer and that it worked to learn from its mistakes. Bloomberg also reports the 65% quarterly loss.

Policy & Regulation

New York bans AI chatbot toys for five years

Gov. Kathy Hochul signed legislation banning the manufacture and sale of toys powered by AI chatbots, including talking stuffed animals, dolls and robots. NBC News reports the ban starts in February. California signed a four-year ban in September, NBC News reports. Hochul cited toys with few or no parental controls and safety limits that weakened over long conversations. Attorney General Letitia James said her office would enforce the law.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, The Guardian AI, SiliconANGLE AI, nbcnews.com

Products

OpenAI merges ChatGPT Chat and Work, with shared usage meter

OpenAI has begun rolling out a combined Chat and Work experience in ChatGPT to a small group of Pro subscribers. The two will share one usage meter, though Instant model conversations will not count toward the allowance. An OpenAI spokesperson told TechCrunch that most users should not see more frequent resets, but a small fraction of the heaviest users may hit limits sooner. Some subscribers have voiced concern about less access at the same price. OpenAI plans a broader rollout in December, per its support page.

Sources: TechCrunch AI, PYMNTS, Decrypt, Crypto Briefing

Other

Anthropic's Amodei calls report on surrendering free will to AI "absurd"

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei responded on X to an Axios report that some Anthropic employees believed humanity should often hand authority to superintelligent systems. He called the suggestion "absurd" and wrote that handing governance to AI is "a bad idea." Amodei said automating specific tasks such as driving or writing software is a different matter. He also said Anthropic does not expect it to be safe for Claude to build successive frontier models fully autonomously, without meaningful human supervision.

Sources: Axios, TechCrunch AI, The Decoder, Al Jazeera

Products

OpenAI tests Private Inference for business customers

OpenAI is testing Private Inference, an alpha feature, with a small group of business customers, CEO Sam Altman said on X. It uses confidential computing on isolated hardware, and OpenAI says its security can be independently verified. The feature is meant to keep business prompts private while models process them. The employee leading the effort, Shekar, called it a first step. He said OpenAI is adding computing capacity for a larger rollout but gave no date.

Sources: The Decoder, The Next Web, The Defiant, BigGo Finance

The Financial Times reports the EU is preparing to set aside up to €50 billion for a Sovereign Tech Equity Fund making long-term, high-risk investments in AI and quantum companies. Operational details are due in February, according to the FT.
