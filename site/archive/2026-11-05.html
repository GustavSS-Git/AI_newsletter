Business & Funding

OpenAI president says company will be ready to go public in 2027

OpenAI president Greg Brockman said at a Wall Street Journal tech conference that the company would be ready for a 2027 listing, Reuters reported. Asked on stage, he replied "I think so." He also said business customers now account for most of OpenAI's sales, but gave no figure for that share or for total revenue. OpenAI has not set a date for a listing.

Sources: The Next Web, The Wall Street Journal, Reuters, unite.ai

Research

OpenAI pipeline finds 948 Linux kernel privilege-escalation vulnerabilities

OpenAI says it built an autonomous pipeline that found, validated and generated patches for 948 local privilege-escalation vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel. The Hacker News reports the run took 30 hours. Gizmodo reports the pipeline racked up $1.2 million in API costs, and BigGo Finance gives $1.21 million. Outlets differ on the run's length: GBHackers News says 10 days.

Sources: OpenAI, The Hacker News, CoinDesk, Help Net Security

Business & Funding

OpenAI to acquire developer tooling startup Pierre Computer

OpenAI announced plans to acquire Pierre Computer Company, which runs a cloud service where AI agents store code. Terms were not disclosed, and The Decoder reports the deal still needs regulatory approval. Pierre's open-source libraries Trees and Diffs already power the interface of OpenAI's Codex coding tool. OpenAI says it will keep them open source and plans to integrate Pierre's paid code hosting features into Codex.

Sources: SiliconANGLE AI, The Decoder, Axios, Dealroom

Products

ChatGPT adds animated GIF creation with Images 2.6 model

ChatGPT can now create animated GIFs from a text prompt or an uploaded photo. Users can adjust animation speed and edit individual frames. The feature comes with OpenAI's new Images 2.6 model, which is rolling out to all ChatGPT and Codex users on web, desktop and mobile. OpenAI also launched API models GPT-Image-2.6 Flare and Sunburst, and says users generate over 4 billion images per week, up from 3 billion in September.

Sources: The Verge AI, The Decoder, The Neuron, trendingtopics.eu

Cat Wu, former Claude Code product lead, joins OpenAI

Cat Wu, who left Anthropic last week, is joining OpenAI to lead product management for core products, according to Business Insider. She announced the move on X. Wu was the product lead for Anthropic's Claude Code.

Sources: Business Insider, Vocal, TradingView, unite.ai

Business & Funding

Microsoft rejects option to expand Nscale's Texas data center deal

Microsoft declined an option to more than double its Texas data center contract with Nscale, the Financial Times reports, citing sources. The option covered a second 700-megawatt phase at the Barstow site from late 2027. The existing Texas contract stays in place. Nscale is seeking a valuation of up to $35 billion in its New York IPO.

Sources: The Next Web, Financial Times, tech.eu

Europe

Kabilio raises €10 million for AI agents in accounting

Barcelona startup Kabilio raised €10 million in a round led by Picus Capital and Visionaries, bringing total funding to €14 million. The company says it will use the money to build an AI agent for accounting firms, which enters beta this month.

Sources: The Next Web, tech.eu

Outlets report on EU AI Act deadlines and transparency rules

SC Media and IT Security Guru report that the EU AI Act's rules, including transparency requirements, are now applying to organizations. Details of the specific deadlines were not available in open sources.

Sources: The European Times, SC Media, IT Security Guru, shattered.io

Business & Finance

Revolut cuts AI coding costs about 90% with open models

Revolut says it cut AI programming costs by about 90% by moving from commercial services to Z.ai's open GLM model on its own infrastructure, The Register reports via Computer Sweden. The company says coding time improved 60% and development lead times 70%.

Sources: Computer Sweden
